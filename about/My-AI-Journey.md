# My AI Journey

## Why This Repository Exists

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping how organizations operate, compete, and grow.

My professional background spans:

- Business Development
- Commercial Real Estate
- Strategic Partnerships
- Digital Transformation

Over the years, I recognized that AI is not merely a technology trend.

It is a transformational business capability.

This repository was created to document practical frameworks, executive playbooks, and real-world use cases that help organizations unlock value from AI.

## Mission

To make AI accessible, actionable, and impactful for business leaders and decision makers.

## Vision

A future where business strategy and AI work together to create sustainable growth and competitive advantage.
