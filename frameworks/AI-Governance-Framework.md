# AI Governance Framework

## Objective

Provide a structured approach for responsible AI adoption.

---

## Governance Pillars

### Leadership

Responsibilities:

- Executive sponsorship
- Strategic oversight
- Risk management

---

### People

Focus Areas:

- Training
- Awareness
- Accountability

---

### Data

Requirements:

- Data Quality
- Security
- Privacy

---

### Technology

Requirements:

- Reliability
- Monitoring
- Integration

---

### Compliance

Focus Areas:

- Regulations
- Policies
- Ethical Standards

---

## Governance Checklist

### Before Deployment

- Business objective defined
- Risk assessment completed
- Stakeholder approval obtained

### After Deployment

- Performance monitored
- Risks reviewed
- Outcomes measured
