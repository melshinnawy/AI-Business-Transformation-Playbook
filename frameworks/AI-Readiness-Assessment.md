# AI Readiness Assessment Framework

## Purpose

This framework helps organizations evaluate their readiness to adopt Artificial Intelligence and identify opportunities for business value creation.

---

## Strategic Alignment

### Questions

- Is AI aligned with business objectives?
- Does leadership support AI initiatives?
- Are measurable outcomes defined?

Score: 1-5

---

## Data Maturity

### Questions

- Is data accessible?
- Is data accurate and reliable?
- Are governance processes established?

Score: 1-5

---

## Technology Infrastructure

### Questions

- Can existing systems support AI deployment?
- Are cloud services available?
- Are integration capabilities adequate?

Score: 1-5

---

## Talent & Skills

### Questions

- Are employees AI-aware?
- Do teams understand prompt engineering?
- Are training programs available?

Score: 1-5

---

## Governance & Risk

### Questions

- Is responsible AI policy defined?
- Are security controls implemented?
- Are regulatory requirements understood?

Score: 1-5

---

## Readiness Levels

### Emerging
Score 0-20

### Developing
Score 21-40

### Advanced
Score 41-60

### Leading
Score 61-80

---

## Recommendation

Organizations should focus on people, process, and technology simultaneously to maximize AI adoption success.
