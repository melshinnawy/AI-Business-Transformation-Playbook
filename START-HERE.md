# Start Here

Welcome to the AI Business Transformation Playbook.

This repository is built around one core idea:

> **The Shinnawy AI Transformation Model™** — a five-phase methodology that turns AI from a technology initiative into a measurable business advantage.

Use this page to find your path based on your role and objective.

---

## If You Are a CEO or Executive Sponsor

1. Complete the [Executive AI Readiness Assessment™](frameworks/executive-ai-readiness-assessment/)
2. Study the [Shinnawy AI Transformation Model™](frameworks/shinnawy-ai-transformation-model/)
3. Review [Executive Scenarios](executive-scenarios/) relevant to your industry

**Time to first value:** ~20 minutes

---

## If You Lead Transformation (CIO, COO, VP Strategy)

1. Study the [Shinnawy AI Transformation Model™](frameworks/shinnawy-ai-transformation-model/)
2. Map your organization using the [AI Operating Model Canvas™](frameworks/ai-operating-model-canvas/)
3. Define ROI using the [AI Value Realization Framework™](frameworks/ai-value-realization-framework/)

**Time to first value:** ~45 minutes
